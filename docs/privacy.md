# omakey privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-16_

omakey is a keyboard, so it sees what you type. This page says what it does with that.

## What stays on your phone

Everything you type, the words omakey learns from you, your clipboard history and your settings
are stored only on your device. omakey does not send them to the developer, to any server, or to
any third party. There are no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting and no tracking
libraries in the app.

- **Learned words** are kept in a local database. You can review and delete them in
  Settings → Privacy & data.
- **Clipboard history** is optional. Clips an app marks as sensitive (passwords, mostly) are never
  saved. Nothing is saved while incognito mode is on or a password field is focused. You can clear
  the history or switch it off in Settings → Privacy & data.
- **Incognito mode** stops omakey learning from what you type.

## What uses the network

One thing: **language packs.** Languages other than English are separate downloads. Nothing is
fetched until you tap "Get more languages" in Settings → Languages and choose one. The download
comes from omakey's public GitHub releases over HTTPS. Packs contain only data (a keyboard layout,
word lists, a language model), never code, and are verified against a signed list before they are
installed. The request does not include anything you typed or any other data about you. As with any
web request, GitHub can see your IP address.

The sideloaded build from GitHub Releases can also check GitHub for a newer app version. The Google
Play build does not.

## Permissions

- **Internet**: only for the language-pack download above.
- **Vibrate**: key-press haptic feedback.
- **Notifications** (sideloaded build only): the optional "update available" notice.

## Children

omakey is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
