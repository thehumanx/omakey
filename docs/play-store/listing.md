## Full description (≤4000)
omakey is the keyboard for people who type like they mean it. Fast, gesture-driven, and private:
nothing you type ever leaves your phone.

• Gesture typing and swipe gestures, undo/redo, and quick edits
• Smart autocorrect and next-word prediction that learn on-device
• Clipboard manager with history (never saves sensitive or incognito input)
• Emoji panel, number pad, symbols page, autofill suggestions
• Themes, including a builder that matches a whole theme to one colour
• Layouts: classic and all-caps; floating keyboard
• Languages: English built in; Spanish, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Russian and Nepali
  as optional downloads
• No ads, no analytics, no accounts. Open source (GPL-3.0): github.com/thehumanx/omakey

After installing, enable omakey in Settings → System → Languages & input → On-screen keyboard.
