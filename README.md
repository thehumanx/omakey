# Omakey

**A fast, gesture-driven Android keyboard, built for people who type a lot.**

</div>

---

## What is Omakey?

Omakey is a modern Android keyboard inspired by the beloved (and now discontinued) Fleksy
keyboard — the one keyboard that made typing feel *fast*. It's built from the ground up with
gestures, speed, and privacy in mind.

If you ever loved a keyboard that let you delete a word with a swipe instead of holding
backspace, add a space with a flick instead of a tap, and just *fly* through a message — Omakey
is for you.

## Why Omakey?

Most keyboards today are bloated, tracking-heavy, or just plain slow. Omakey takes a different
approach:

- ⚡ **Gesture-first typing.** Swipe left to delete a word, swipe right to add a space, swipe
  up or down to cycle through word suggestions. No more hunting for tiny buttons — your whole
  keyboard becomes the gesture surface.
- 🔒 **Privacy by design.** Omakey doesn't send what you type anywhere. No analytics, no ad
  SDKs, no tracking. What you type stays on your device.
- 🪶 **Light and fast.** No bloated feature creep, no background services draining your
  battery. Just a keyboard that gets out of your way.
- 🧩 **Extensions, not clutter.** Clipboard history and a full modern emoji picker are built
  right in, accessible from one dedicated key — instead of every feature living permanently on
  your screen.
- 🎨 **Make it yours.** Multiple themes, adjustable keyboard height, custom fonts, and
  fine-tunable haptic/sound feedback so typing feels exactly the way you want it to.

## Features

### Typing that keeps up with you
- Full QWERTY layout with shift/caps, symbols, and long-press accent characters (à, é, ñ, and
  more).
- A tap-preview bubble on every key press, so you always know what you hit.
- Tuned for fast typists — built to keep up even when your fingers overlap mid-word.

### Gestures that just work
- **Swipe left** — delete the last word.
- **Swipe right** — insert a space.
- **Swipe up / down** — cycle through word suggestions, or save a new word to your personal
  dictionary.
- Adjustable gesture sensitivity in Settings, so it fits your typing style, not the other way
  around.

### Smart suggestions and autocorrect
- Real autocorrect — typos are fixed automatically as you type, not just offered as a suggestion
  you have to tap. Got corrected wrong? One tap of backspace undoes it.
- Next-word prediction that's genuinely useful from the first sentence you type, not just after
  weeks of use.
- A swipeable strip above the keyboard — flick between suggestions, quick text tools (select
  all, copy, cut, paste, clipboard history), and a numbers row, all in the same space.

### A full, modern emoji picker
- Thousands of emoji across every standard category — smileys, people, animals, food, travel,
  activities, objects, symbols, and flags — plus a dedicated special-characters picker for
  things like °, ™, §, arrows, and math symbols.

### Feedback that feels right
- Adjustable haptic feedback with a strength slider.
- A satisfying keypress sound you can toggle on or off.

### Make it feel like yours
- Multiple built-in themes (light, dark, and more).
- Adjustable keyboard height.
- Custom font options for your keys.
- A home-row highlight to help you find your place by feel.

### Accessible
- Automatic fallback for TalkBack users — when screen-reader touch exploration is on, Omakey
  switches to standard tap-to-type instead of gesture capture, so nothing gets in the way of
  accessibility tools.

## Privacy

Omakey does not request internet access. Nothing you type is ever transmitted anywhere — there's
no server for it to go to. Your personal dictionary, clipboard history, and settings all live
only on your device.

## Status

Omakey just hit its first proper release, 1.0.0. Core typing, gestures, autocorrect, predictive
text, themes, and extensions are all working today. New features are landing regularly — see
[CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) for what's new in each release.

## Getting started

Omakey isn't yet published to the Play Store. In the meantime, grab the latest APK from the
[Releases page](https://github.com/thehumanx/omakey/releases) and sideload it, or build from
source.

After installing:
1. Open your phone's **Settings → System → Languages & input → On-screen keyboard**, and enable
   Omakey.
2. Switch to it from the keyboard-switcher icon on your existing keyboard, or from the same
   Settings screen.
